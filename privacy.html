We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services or contact us. This includes:

Social Media Login Data. We offer Google sign-in. If you use this, we receive your name and email address from Google.

Google API. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We process your personal information for the following reasons:

  • To deliver services to you — to provide the AI study tools you request.
  • To respond to your inquiries — to answer questions and provide support.
  • To send administrative information — to send details about changes to our terms and policies.
  • To protect vital interests — to prevent harm where necessary.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Performance of a Contract — to fulfil our contractual obligations to provide you the service.
  • Legal Obligations — to comply with applicable laws.
  • Vital Interests — to protect safety where necessary.

If you are in Canada, we process your information where you have given express or implied consent, or where permitted by law without consent.

Our servers are located in the United States and United Kingdom. Your information may be transferred to and processed in these countries. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we use the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure your data is protected in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

We keep your personal information for as long as you have an account with us. When you terminate your account, we will delete or anonymise your information from our active databases, though we may retain some information to prevent fraud or comply with legal requirements.

9. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We use appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information. You should only access the Services within a secure environment.
